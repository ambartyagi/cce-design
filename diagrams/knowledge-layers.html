CCE · ADR-0015 platform tenants

How EazeWork knowledge is layered: product-wide vs. company-specific

EazeWork is one platform tenant whose knowledge every customer company can read. Each customer company is a private client tenant. Postgres row-level security enforces this, not just the application code.

Where this stands

  • Live: platform/client tenants, read-only inheritance enforced by RLS, integrator key with X-CCE-Tenant, ordering by owner, and owner on every citation (migration 0005, 10 walking-skeleton checks).
  • Not yet platform-level: statutory rules such as EPF and gratuity are still copied into each company by country when the pack installs. Moving them to the platform layer is on the roadmap.
  • Opt-out: a tenant with inherits_platform = false sees no platform rows. Eval tenants use this so the golden set stays hermetic.
  • Exclusions: configuration gaps are signals for certifiers and are never cited in answers. Confidential rows reach privileged roles only.
